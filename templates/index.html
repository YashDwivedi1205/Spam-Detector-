How accurate is this?

The checker learned from about 10,000 older English emails and text messages. The results below come from 2,033 messages it had never seen. It can miss newer scams, such as delivery-fee or job-offer messages, and it gives a probability, not a guarantee.

Train the model

Training reads the labelled messages from MongoDB, learns from 80% of them and tests on the other 20%. It runs in the background, so you can keep checking messages while it works.
